## Brand & Style

This design system channels the sun-soaked warmth of an Athenian late afternoon dissolving into an electric, neon-drenched night. Rooted in Ilioupoli ("Sun City"), it captures the playful, magnetic energy of three female friends hosting an eternal aperitivo hour. The personality is sun-drenched, unapologetic, chic, and kinetic—straddling daytime spritz culture and late-night DJ sets.

The visual style is **Warm Nostalgic Editorial meets Electric 90s/00s Club Culture**. It pairs high-contrast, Italian editorial luxury (Didone flourishes, razor-sharp serifs) with tactile printed ephemera: adhesive cardboard labels, hand-scrawled drink specials, receipt cutouts, and intense hyper-saturated neon glows. The interface balances clean, editorial space with vivid, saturated bursts of citrus and nocturnal club gradients.

## Colors

The palette operates across two emotional shifts: **Aperitivo Golden Hour** and **Electric Mezzanotte**.

### Color Roles
- **Primary (`#E85C1F` - Tangerine Aperitivo):** The signature vibrant burnt-orange liquor hue. Used for primary CTAs, bold accent tags, hero highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#E8246C` - Electric Campari Neon):** Punchy club magenta-pink. Drives late-night event markers, celebratory micro-interactions, and highlight states.
- **Tertiary (`#7928CA` - Club Ultraviolet):** Deep neon violet. Works in tandem with `#E8246C` to build evening gradients, nocturnal badges, and vinyl session schedules.
- **Neutral (`#1A1412` - Espresso Nero):** Warm-undertone near-black espresso. Used for high-contrast headlines, crisp body text, and structural outlines. Replaces cold, sterile blacks.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Light: `#FFFDF9` (Linen Spritz Paper).
  - Elevated Container: `#F6F0E6` (Aperitivo Cardboard / Biscuit).
  - Nocturnal Surface: `#120E0D` (Sub-basement Nero with electric magenta-violet under-glow).

### Gradient Specifications
- **Aperitivo Dusk:** Linear gradient (135deg, `#E85C1F` 0%, `#E8246C` 100%).
- **Electric Mezzanotte:** Linear gradient (135deg, `#E8246C` 0%, `#7928CA` 60%, `#9333EA` 100%).

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial balance: Italian high-fashion contrast meeting modern geometric utility.

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers sharp, vertical Didone elegance with dramatic stroke contrast. Perfect for bilingual Greek and Latin typographic lockups, giving bottle names, event lineups, and section headers an editorial luxury magazine feel. Use italic variants for expressive accents (e.g., *Spritz Speciale*, *DJ Sets*).
- **Body & UI Controls (`Outfit`):** Clean, geometric, open apertures ensure crisp readability on digital menus, ingredient descriptions, and reservation forms.
- **Accents & Stickers:** Small capsule tags, pricing labels, and cardboard stickers use all-caps, tracked-out label styling, frequently rotated slightly (-2deg to +3deg) to invoke physical coasters and taped labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a rhythmic **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and tablet viewports, transitioning to a **4-column responsive layout** on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile:** `< 768px` (4 columns, `16px` gutters, `20px` side margins).
- **Tablet:** `768px - 1024px` (8 columns, `24px` gutters, `32px` side margins).
- **Desktop:** `> 1024px` (12 columns, `32px` gutters, `56px` side margins; max content container `1320px`).

### Layout Model
Layouts prioritize generous negative space along editorial vertical rhythms, interspersed with overlapping elements—such as drink photos breaking column boundaries or sticker badges pinning themselves over card edges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tactile paper layering** and **colored diffuse ambiance** rather than generic grey drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Sun Surface):** `#FFFDF9`. Completely flat paper background with a warm grain feel.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Coasters):** `#F6F0E6` surface framed by a hairline `1px solid rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.08)` outline. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(232, 92, 31, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Flying Menus, Floating Totes):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an offset shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.16)`.
- **Electric Night Overlays (Late-Night DJ / Event mode):** Dark tinted backdrop blur (`rgba(18, 14, 13, 0.85)` with `16px` blur), topped with radiant rim lighting using `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(232, 36, 108, 0.45), 0 0 80px -20px rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft-to-pill hybrid** approach:

- Standard structural containers, menu tiles, and cards use rounded corners (`0.5rem` to `1rem`), evoking pressed cocktail napkins and bar menus.
- Interactive CTAs, pricing capsules, status chips, and tag pills use fully rounded pills (`9999px`) reflecting bottle caps, citrus slices, and soda labels.
- Occasional decorative sticker elements retain asymmetrical corner treatments (e.g., `rounded-tl-none rounded-br-2xl`) to emphasize a scrapbook, DIY bar culture feel.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary ("Aperitivo Push"):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#E85C1F` background, `#FFFFFF` text, high tracking on labels. On hover, shifts to an active gradient (`#E85C1F` to `#E8246C`) with a gentle transform `translateY(-2px)`.
- **Secondary ("Electric Club"):** Pill-shaped, transparent background with `2px solid #1A1412` or neon gradient border, high-contrast text. On hover, fills with `#E8246C` and white text.
- **Ghost Action:** Underlined Bodoni italic with a custom squiggly orange accent on hover.

### Chips, Stickers & Badges
- **Sticker Chips:** Slightly rotated (`-2deg`), light cardboard background (`#F6F0E6`), solid `1.5px` border in `#1A1412`, featuring uppercase label styling.
- **Nightlife Glow Badges:** Gradient fill (`#E8246C` to `#7928CA`) with white text and a diffuse neon shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(232, 36, 108, 0.4)`).

### Menu & Spritz Cards
- **Product Card:** Light ecru background, crisp hairline border, rounded corners (`1rem`). Card top features high-ratio photography; bottom hosts the cocktail title in `Bodoni Moda`, bitter/sweet notes in `Outfit`, and the price contained within an orange capsule chip.
- **DJ / Event Card:** Inverts to dark surface (`#120E0D`), featuring magenta-to-purple gradient borders, glowing typography, and embedded audio player widgets.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Cream background (`#F6F0E6`), border `1px solid rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.2)`, `0.5rem` radius. On focus, transitions to `border-color: #E85C1F` with a soft orange ring shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 92, 31, 0.2)`.
- **Radio & Checkbox:** Tangerine `#E85C1F` fill when active, paired with espresso borders when resting.
